# PodiumPanda

**Make room for music.** PodiumPanda helps orchestras and ensembles prepare concerts together, with a shared workspace and handy tools for musicians.

[Open PodiumPanda](https://podiumpanda.com)

## Plan together

Create a group, add your players and organize multiple concerts in one place. Share a link with your ensemble—no accounts needed.

- **Setlists:** plan the music for each concert, including parts and links to recordings.
- **Preferences and assignments:** collect players' preferences and decide who plays each part.
- **Stage plans:** arrange chairs, stands and instruments on editable floorplans, and export them as images.
- **Concert guests:** add players for a single concert without changing the group roster.
- **Music player:** listen to supported YouTube and Spotify links in the app, or open other links externally.

Changes are shared with the group, and the latest shared edit can be undone. Anyone with the group link can view and edit its contents, so share it carefully; links cannot currently be revoked or rotated.

## Tools for musicians

- **Metronome:** set the tempo and time signature, adjust subdivisions, or tap a tempo.
- **Tuner:** check a sustained note with your microphone, see how sharp or flat it is, and choose a reference pitch and instrument. Audio stays on your device.
- **Tone generator:** play a tone, adjust its frequency, waveform, volume and balance, and step between notes. It uses the tuner's saved instrument and reference pitch.
- **Transposition:** select a note on a marimba-style keyboard and convert it between instruments, with octave labels and concert pitch. Custom intervals support other instruments.

Start tones at a low volume and avoid prolonged or loud playback. The tuner requires microphone permission and a supported browser on HTTPS or localhost.

## Made for your ensemble

PodiumPanda is available in English and Dutch, with light and dark themes. Group shortcuts and your preferences can be remembered in your browser. Clearing browser storage removes those shortcuts, not the shared groups or concerts.
