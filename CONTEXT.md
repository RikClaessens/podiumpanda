# PodiumPanda

## Language

**Group**:
A musical ensemble that prepares concerts together and maintains its own roster of players.

**Group player**:
A player belonging to a group's roster who is available across that group's concerts.

**Concert**:
A performance prepared by a group, with its own setlist, preferences, assignments and floorplans.

**Concert guest**:
A player added only to a particular concert, rather than to the group's roster.

**Part**:
A role or instrument in a piece that any number of players can be assigned to play. A player's part can differ from one piece to another.

**Player**:
A person in the group or a concert, without a fixed instrument or role.
