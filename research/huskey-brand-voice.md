## Voice and tone

- Personal, first person singular ("Ik", "I"), warm, informal ("Hey"; Dutch uses "je", never "u").
- Plain, concrete, benefit-led; short sentences; verbs of doing (build, collect, assign, arrange, share).
- Light humour/personal touch (Husky story, "full-stack musician"); modest, not corporate or hype-y. No superlatives or buzzwords on the page.
- Recurring values: simplicity ("razendsnel", "fast and painless"), no mandatory accounts, "just works on the day".
- Bilingual NL/EN with parallel, not literal, translations; NL is default.

## Guidance for describing PodiumPanda

1. Lead with the benefit: making music together, made easier / "Samen muziek maken, makkelijker geregeld".
2. Use the verb chain: setlist, preferences, parts, stage; "share one link, no accounts".
3. Keep a friendly, human, maker-to-musician tone; first person is acceptable when authored by Rik, otherwise "you/je".
4. Prefer musician vocabulary (concert, setlist, parts, stage) over software jargon; this matches CONTEXT.md terms (group, concert, part).
5. Mention metronome/tuner only as "handy extras".
6. Do not claim features, pricing, customers or services not listed above.
